### Hartman Creek State Park
Source: <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hartman_Creek_State_Park> (CC BY-SA), retrieved 2026-11-13.

Hartman Creek State Park is a 1,417-acre (573 ha) Wisconsin state park near Waupaca, Wisconsin, United States. The park contains several small lakes. Recreational features include 12 miles of off-road biking trails, 10 miles of hiking trails which include a section of the Ice Age National Scenic Trail, 7 miles of bridal trails, 300-foot sand beach, and campsites in an old apple orchard.
