## 4. Wikipedia text
### Panola Mountain
Source: <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panola_Mountain> (CC BY-SA), retrieved 2026-11-13.

Panola Mountain is a 100-acre (40 ha) granite monadnock in Rockdale County, Georgia. The peak is 946 feet (288 m) above sea level, rising 260 feet (79 m) above the South River. The South River marks the boundary between Henry, Rockdale, and DeKalb counties. Due to its delicate ecological features, Panola Mountain was designated a National Natural Landmark on December 16, 1980.
