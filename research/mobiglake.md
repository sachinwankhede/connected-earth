## 4. Wikipedia text
### Big Lake State Park
Source: <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Lake_State_Park> (CC BY-SA), retrieved 2026-11-13.

Big Lake State Park is a public recreation area located in northwest Missouri, United States. The 407-acre (165 ha) state park was established in 1932 at the northern end of the state's largest oxbow lake, Big Lake. Park activities include boating, camping, picnicking, fishing, and swimming. Because park accommodations have been repeatedly destroyed by Missouri River floods, the park began using wheeled rental cabins that can be moved in the event of flooding in 2016.
