## 4. Wikipedia text
### Dworshak State Park
Source: <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dworshak_State_Park> (CC BY-SA), retrieved 2026-11-13.

Dworshak State Park is a public recreation area covering 850 acres (340 ha) along the western shore of Dworshak Reservoir north of Orofino in Clearwater County, Idaho, United States. The state park comprises three units: Freeman Creek, Three Meadows Group Camp, and Big Eddy Lodge and Marina. There are opportunities for boating, fishing, camping, swimming, water-skiing, and hiking in the park.

== Wildlife ==
Animals that inhabit this state park are deer, kokanee, osprey, black crappie, bass, trout and bald eagles.

== Climate ==

Dworshak's climate is classified as Warm-summer Mediterranean climate (Köppen Csa). Among them, the annual average temperature is 51.7 °F (10.9 °C), the hottest month in July is 72.2 °F (22.3 °C), and the coldest month is 33.3 °F (0.7 °C) in December. The annual precipitation is 26.10 inches (662.9 mm), of which November is the wettest with 3.23 inches (82.0 mm), while August is the driest with only 0.68 inches (17.3 mm). The extreme temperature throughout the year ranged from −22 °F (−30 °C) on January 21, 1937 to 118 °F (48 °C) on July 28, 1934.
