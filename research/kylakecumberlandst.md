## 4. Wikipedia text
### Lake Cumberland State Resort Park
Source: <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Cumberland_State_Resort_Park> (CC BY-SA), retrieved 2026-11-13.

Lake Cumberland State Resort Park is a public recreation area located on the northern shore of Lake Cumberland four miles south of Jamestown in Russell County, Kentucky, United States. The state park itself encompasses 3,117 acres (1,261 ha), while the lake, its major feature, covers approximately 50,250 acres (20,340 ha), which has been reduced to 37,000 acres (15,000 ha). Park offerings include lodging, camping, marina, fishing, and disc golf.
