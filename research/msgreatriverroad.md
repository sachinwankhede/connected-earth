## 4. Wikipedia text
### Great River Road State Park
Source: <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_River_Road_State_Park> (CC BY-SA), retrieved 2026-11-13.

Great River Road State Park is a public recreation area in the U.S. state of Mississippi located off Mississippi Highway 1 in the southwest corner of the city of Rosedale. The day-use state park is managed by the city of Rosedale.

== Activities and amenities ==
The state park features boating and fishing on 25-acre (10 ha) Perry Martin Lake as well as picnicking facilities and a playground. The park's 75-foot (23 m) lookout tower which offered views of the Mississippi River was taken down following the damaging floods of 2011.
