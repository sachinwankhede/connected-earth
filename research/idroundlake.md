## 4. Wikipedia text
### Round Lake State Park
Source: <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Round_Lake_State_Park> (CC BY-SA), retrieved 2026-11-13.

Round Lake State Park is a public recreation area located four miles (6.4 km) southwest of Sagle in Bonner County, Idaho. The 142-acre (57 ha) state park surrounds 55-acre (22 ha) Round Lake. The lake was formed from glacial activity in the Pleistocene. The park offers campsites, fishing, swimming, boating for non-motor and electric-powered watercraft, and trails for hiking, biking and skiing.

== Wildlife ==
This state park is home to black bear, moose, grizzly bear, waterfowl, beaver, various songbirds, and deer.
