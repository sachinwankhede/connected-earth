## 4. Wikipedia text
### Natchez State Park
Source: <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natchez_State_Park> (CC BY-SA), retrieved 2026-11-13.

Natchez State Park is a public recreation area in the U.S. state of Mississippi. The park is located off U.S. Highway 61 near Stanton, ten miles (16 km) northeast of its namesake, Natchez, Mississippi.

== Activities and amenities ==
The park features boating and fishing on Natchez Lake, primitive and developed campsites, ten cabins, a nature trail and equestrian roadways, picnic area, and a 9-hole disc golf course. The state record largemouth bass, 18.15 pounds (8.23 kg), was caught in Natchez Lake in 1992.
