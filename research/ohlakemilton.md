## 4. Wikipedia text
### Lake Milton State Park
Source: <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Milton_State_Park> (CC BY-SA), retrieved 2026-11-13.

Lake Milton State Park is in Lake Milton, Ohio. The 1,700 acres (6.9 km2) state park offers boating, swimming, and fishing as well as archery, disc golf, hinting, bird watching, basketball court, sand volleyball court, and mile-long trails There is also a 10 miles (16 km) mountain biking trail. in the winter snowmobiling, cross-country skiing, and ice fishing are offered.
The lake is formed by a dam.
A storybook trail featuring Raindrops to Rainbow by John Micklos Jr opened in 2022. E. coli has been found in the water over various samplings over ten years. In 2020 a man's body was found in the lake.
