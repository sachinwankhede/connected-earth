## 4. Wikipedia text
### Meade State Park
Source: <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meade_State_Park> (CC BY-SA), retrieved 2026-11-13.

Meade State Park is a state park southwest of the city of Meade in Meade County, Kansas, United States. The park features an 80-acre (32 ha) lake that contains bluegill, channel cat, and largemouth bass. Fishing is allowed in the lake year-round with motorized boating restricted to fishing only and no wake speed. In addition the park encompasses a 440-acre (180 ha) campsite and wildlife area with hiking trails.
