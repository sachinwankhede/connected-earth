## 4. Wikipedia text
### Lone Mesa State Park
Source: <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lone_Mesa_State_Park> (CC BY-SA), retrieved 2026-11-13.

Lone Mesa State Park is a closed-access state park in Colorado located 23 miles (37 kilometers) north of Dolores. It is currently undergoing development and planning. According to the park's website, the park is "restricted to hunting, volunteer and educational programs."
Several local endemic species have been located or described in the park including Physaria puvinata  Gutierrezia elegens and Pakera mancosana.
