## 4. Wikipedia text
### Fall River State Park
Source: <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fall_River_State_Park> (CC BY-SA), retrieved 2026-11-13.

Fall River State Park is an 1,107-acre (448 ha) public recreation area in Greenwood County, Kansas, United States, southwest of the city of Toronto. Located near the Flint Hills, the state park features six different hiking trails: Casner Creek, Turkey Run, Post Oak, Overlook, Bluestem, and Catclaw. Other activities include camping, picnicking, swimming, boating and waterskiing on the 2,450-acre (990 ha) Fall River Lake for which the state park is named.
