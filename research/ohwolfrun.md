## 4. Wikipedia text
### Wolf Run State Park
Source: <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolf_Run_State_Park> (CC BY-SA), retrieved 2026-11-13.

Wolf Run State Park is a 1,338-acre (541 ha) public recreation area located three miles north of the village of Caldwell, Ohio, in the United States. The state park features hiking on trails that include a section of the Buckeye Trail plus swimming, boating and fishing on 220-acre (89 ha) Wolf Run Lake. The Noble County Airport is located nearby.
