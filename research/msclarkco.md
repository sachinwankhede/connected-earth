## 4. Wikipedia text
### Clarkco State Park
Source: <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clarkco_State_Park> (CC BY-SA), retrieved 2026-11-13.

Clarkco State Park is a public recreation area in the U.S. state of Mississippi located off Mississippi Highway 145 (old U.S. Highway 45), five miles (8.0 km) north of Quitman.

== Activities and amenities ==
The state park features boating, waterskiing, and fishing on a 65-acre (26 ha) lake, campsites, camper cabins and cottages. Other amenities include a visitors center, a disc golf course, a picnic area, and nature trails.
