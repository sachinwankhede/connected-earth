## 4. Wikipedia text
### Mooween State Park
Source: <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mooween_State_Park> (CC BY-SA), retrieved 2026-11-13.

Mooween State Park is a public recreation area covering 577 acres (234 ha) in the town of Lebanon, Connecticut. The state park offers hiking and mountain biking plus fishing and boating on 127-acre (51 ha) Red Cedar Lake.

== History ==
The park grounds were the home of a summer camp for boys from 1921 through 1960. The state purchased the site in 1989. Originally known as Red Cedar Lake State Park, its name was changed to Mooween in 2000 in recognition of its summer camp past.
