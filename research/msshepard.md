## 4. Wikipedia text
### Shepard State Park
Source: <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shepard_State_Park> (CC BY-SA), retrieved 2026-11-13.

Shepard State Park is a public recreation area located off U.S. Highway 90 on the south side of the city of Gautier, Mississippi. The city has had responsibility for the state park's management since 2013.

== Activities and amenities ==
The park features boating and fishing on the Singing River, tent camping, developed RV campsites, 8 miles (13 km) of nature trails, playing fields, picnic area, playground, and an 18-hole disc golf course, Alligator Alley.
