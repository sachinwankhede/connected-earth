## Mount Diablo State Park (`camountdiablo`)

**Story** (27 words): Mount Diablo State Park is located in California, USA, within the eastern San Francisco Bay Area. The forest covers about 20,000 acres around an isolated upthrust peak.

**Why it matters** (22 words): The protected land is part of a larger network of public preserves totaling more than 90,000 acres on and around the mountain.

## Mount San Jacinto State Park (`camountsanjacinto`)

**Story** (31 words): Mount San Jacinto State Park is in California, USA, in the San Jacinto Mountains. It encompasses the weathered granite summit of Mount San Jacinto, which reaches 10,834 feet above sea level.

**Why it matters** (30 words): Part of the natural area is old-growth forest recognized by the Old-Growth Forest Network. A majority of the park is within the Santa Rosa and San Jacinto Mountains National Monument.

## Mount Tamalpais State Park (`camounttamalpais`)

**Story** (29 words): Mount Tamalpais State Park is located in Marin County, California, and features the 2,571 feet Mount Tamalpais. The mountain covers around 25,000 acres of mostly redwood and oak forests.

**Why it matters** (31 words): The park has about 60 miles of hiking trails connected to a larger, 200-mile network of trails in neighboring public lands. Muir Woods National Monument is surrounded by the state park.

## Pacheco State Park (`capacheco`)

**Story** (41 words): Pacheco State Park is a forest located in California, USA, containing 6,890 acres in the Diablo Range. It is known for its vistas of the Central Valley and the Santa Clara Valley, and features impressive displays of colorful wildflowers in spring.

**Why it matters** (31 words): Wildlife in the park includes tule elk, deer, bobcat, coyote, fox, golden eagles, and several varieties of hawks. Park land is also used for cattle grazing during the winter and spring.

## Point Mugu State Park (`capointmugu`)

**Story** (46 words): Point Mugu State Park is located in the Santa Monica Mountains National Recreation Area in Southern California. It features oceanfront beaches, chaparral-covered mountains, and two major canyons with seasonal rivers. The park adjoins Mugu Lagoon, which is a protected area within Naval Air Station Point Mugu.

**Why it matters** (25 words): The western terminus of the Backbone Trail, a multi-use long-distance trail extending nearly 70 miles across the Santa Monica Mountains, is located in the park.

## Prairie Creek Redwoods State Park (`caprairiecreekredw`)

**Story** (35 words): Prairie Creek Redwoods State Park is a coastal sanctuary in California for old-growth Coast Redwood trees. Created in 1923, the 57-square-kilometre park is home to Roosevelt elk, the tailed frog, and several species of salmon.

**Why it matters** (36 words): The park is jointly managed as part of the Redwood National and State Parks, which has been collectively designated as a World Heritage Site and forms a part of the California Coast Ranges International Biosphere Reserve.

## Red Rock Canyon State Park (`caredrockcanyon`)

**Story** (26 words): Red Rock Canyon State Park is a forest located in California, USA, specifically in Kern County, California. It shelters species such as the oneseedjuniper and elk.

**Why it matters** (22 words): The park provides a habitat for recorded species within the USA and belongs to a wider region with various canyons and parks.

## Silver Strand State Beach (`casilverstrandstat`)

**Story** (34 words): Silver Strand State Beach is located in California, USA, in San Diego County. It features a coastline trimmed with silver shells and is a popular recreational destination for camping, fishing, swimming, surfing, and volleyball.

**Why it matters** (19 words): Together with the Point Loma peninsula, Silver Strand shelters and defines San Diego Bay, connecting Coronado with Imperial Beach.

## Sonoma Coast State Park (`casonomacoast`)

**Story** (36 words): Sonoma Coast State Park is located in California, USA, running for approximately 17 miles to Bodega Head. The park features sandy beaches, rocky shoreline, sea stacks, and a marine terrace with upland California coastal prairie habitat.

**Why it matters** (33 words): The park conserves marine ecosystems and ocean and freshwater wildlife through marine protected areas. It is connected to the adjacent Ocean Song preserve, which expands the protected area for wildlife and natural vegetation.

## The Forest of Nisene Marks State Park (`catheforestofnisen`)

**Story** (46 words): The Forest of Nisene Marks State Park is located in California, United States, protecting a secondary forest across 10,223 acres of variable terrain. Four-fifths of the park is covered in dense redwood forest, rising from sea level to steep coastal mountains of more than 2,600 feet.

**Why it matters** (27 words): The park protects a secondary forest in the watershed of Aptos Creek and Soquel Creek within the Santa Cruz Mountains, featuring an ancient grove of redwood trees.

## Topanga State Park (`catopanga`)

**Story** (46 words): Topanga State Park is located in the Santa Monica Mountains within Los Angeles County, California. Covering 11,000 acres, it is considered the largest park located in the limits of a city. The park features sedimentary sandstone rock formations, coastal sage scrub, and montane chaparral and woodlands.

**Why it matters** (29 words): The park is part of the Santa Monica Mountains National Recreation Area and is home to over eighty mammal species as well as many migratory and resident bird species.

## Wilder Ranch State Park (`cawilderranch`)

**Story** (45 words): Wilder Ranch State Park is a California State Park on the Pacific Ocean coast north of Santa Cruz, California. The park totals 7,000 acres and extends from the shore to the town of Felton. Many ranch buildings have been restored for use as a museum.

**Why it matters** (25 words): Natural Bridges State Marine Reserve is a marine protected area along the coast of the park that helps conserve ocean tidal-zone wildlife and marine ecosystems.

## Barr Lake State Park (`cobarrlake`)

**Story** (30 words): Barr Lake State Park covers 2,715 acres in Colorado, USA, and was established in 1977. Numerous bald eagles spend the winter at the park and one pair nests every year.

**Why it matters** (26 words): Over 350 bird species have been observed at the park, which is popular with birders and features a southern half that serves as a wildlife refuge.

## Castlewood Canyon State Park (`cocastlewoodcanyon`)

**Story** (37 words): Castlewood Canyon State Park is a Colorado state park near Franktown, encompassing 2,136 acres within the northernmost extension of the Black Forest. It contains the remains of the historic Castlewood Canyon Dam and the Cherry Creek Bridge.

**Why it matters** (25 words): The park is on the edge of the Palmer Divide, where more moisture falls to water the Black Forest and support a nature preservation area.

## Chatfield State Park (`cochatfield`)

**Story** (41 words): Chatfield State Park is located in Douglas and Jefferson counties of Colorado, United States. The park centers on Chatfield Reservoir, which has a 1,423 acre surface area lake. Over three hundred bird species and a variety of mammals roam the park.

**Why it matters** (35 words): The park hosts over three hundred bird species, both migrant and resident, that frequent the area. The Audubon Society of Greater Denver operates a nature education center here with an emphasis on children and families.
