# Journeys (next to build)

Today: a Places | Journeys tab with compact route cards (13 journeys, hand-written, in `data/links.json` under `cross`). No count on the tab.

## Dedicated journey page (wanted soon)
Opens like a food web does, from a journey card. Contents:
1. Header: route and photo, e.g. "Osprey · Yellowstone → Amazon".
2. The story: 3-4 short lines on why it travels (food, breeding, weather) and what happens if it is lost.
3. The map: the route as an arc, a "Fly the route" button, and when it travels (e.g. leaves in autumn, returns in spring).
4. Both ends: a small card for each place with the species' spot in its food web and a button to open it.
5. Sources behind the story.
6. A next journey (related ones, e.g. other birds on the same route).

## Finding more journeys (13 is too few)
1. Candidates from the data: species present in places more than ~2,500 km apart, kept only if listed as migratory (UN migratory-species convention, Wikipedia, Wikidata). Residents such as deer, coyote and raven are not journeys; monarch, bald eagle, mallard, Canada goose and turkey vulture are.
2. Nutrient flows by hand: salmon into forests, whale falls, seabird guano, river silt to deltas, Saharan dust.
3. Each journey written from cited sources, one sentence per place.
4. Review in batches of about 20.
