# Connected Earth: design rules

One place for the UX rules, so the app feels like one app. Read this before any UI work and update it when a rule changes. If a screen needs to break a rule, change the rule here first.

UX design is two files: this one (principles, foundations, components, screens, behaviour) and `docs/DESIGN-SEARCH.md` (how search and species behave: flows, states, matching rules, decided points). Read both for any search or species screen.

## Principles
1. **Consistency over novelty.** New designs are welcome, but the same thing looks and behaves the same everywhere.
2. **Mobile and desktop stay in sync.** Only layout-dependent parts differ; every improvement applies to both.
3. **Self-explanatory.** No "tap this" hints, no jargon. If it needs a hint, redesign it.
4. **Mobile screen space is premium.** Prefer one line over a card, and nothing permanent that covers content.
5. **Avoid buttons.** Prefer plain links, and tappable cards or rows with a chevron.
6. **Photos, not icons,** wherever a photo exists. Icons only as the fallback while a photo loads or is missing.

## Foundations
How everything looks and reads, on every screen.
- **Fonts.** Display font for names and titles, body font for text, mono for small labels. Sizes: page title 24, row title 16, body 16, card name 12–13, label 11, fine print 12.
- **Capitals.** Capital letters are forced (`text-transform: uppercase`) in three roles only: short kind labels and badges (`.tag`, `.badge`, `.tlpill`, `.jkind`, `.eyebrow`), section heading labels (`h3`/`h4`, "Found in") and map labels. Everything else is plain text in normal case: the count and note lines (`.sub`), one-line row descriptions, hints, counts, and the More and Sources & credits links. A new style must not add capitals outside those roles. (#481, owner: be frugal)
- **Text length.** On a place page, "The story" is at most 60 words and "Why it matters" at most 40, so both fit a phone screen without scrolling. Anything longer goes in the sources, not the text. `tests/textlength.test.mjs` enforces it.
- **Place summary.** A state park or refuge with no story shows one plain sentence from the first line of its Wikipedia article (no lead-in, same spot and look as the story). At most 40 words, with brackets removed, and none when the article is a disambiguation page or about something else. The Wikipedia article is credited in the place's Sources & credits. `tests/textlength.test.mjs` enforces it.
- **Start screen.** Logo, name, tagline and a thin progress bar on a dark ocean blue (`#boot`, `#0b2230`) with light text, in light and dark mode alike, close to the satellite map's opening look so the hand-over to the map is not a flash from white. Removed (fade) when the map has drawn.
- **Story blocks.** "The story" is plain text with a bold lead-in; "Why it matters" has the accent bar (`.jwhy`). Journeys and places use the same blocks.

## Components
Reused across screens. Reuse the class; do not write new CSS.
- **Back.** One link: accent colour, 16px / 600, "‹ " plus the name of where it goes ("‹ Places", "‹ Journeys", "‹ Greater Yellowstone"). Explore always goes back up to its place page, even after a refresh. 44px tall, top-left of the content on every screen: 24px from the edge on a phone; on desktop the edge of the page column (journeys, places) or of the full-width graph (explore). Over the graph it gets a faint backdrop. Never an arrow-only icon, never a ✕. Code: `.jback` (pages) and `#pbk` (explore); both must look identical (a test checks it).
- **No ✕ on the species sheet.** It closes by tapping empty graph, pressing Esc, or the chevron on phone. A ✕, if ever needed, only dismisses something on top of a screen and never navigates.
- **Clear in the search box.** A small ✕ inside the right end of the field, shown only when there is text, 44px tap area; it empties the field and keeps focus. It is the one ✕ that is not on a sheet, because it navigates nowhere and every phone user expects it there (#409).
- **Result header.** One muted line above the list (`#qhead`) when the search text is exactly one species or place: bold name, "found in N places" and a plain link "Species page ›", or "Open the place ›". After "Show on the map" it is the filter chip: "Places with <b>Osprey</b> ✕" (the ✕ is a link that clears the search). A card of a species' place carries one muted line "Has osprey". Links, not buttons. (#425)
- **Result row.** Places, Species and Journeys use one row in lists and search results: 52px square picture (or the fallback icon) on the left, the title (16px, 700) first, one muted line below in plain text (not capitals), a chevron on the right. A Places row has a second muted line, "N species · N feeding links" (#508). Places and Journeys keep their own list container; only the row look is shared. Blurbs, counts and the "Open the food web" link are not in the row. (#483)
- **One list format.** Rows on all three tabs are separate rounded cards: 1px line, 12px corners, 10px padding, 8px between rows, 52px picture, 16px title, one muted line, a chevron. Places, Journeys and Species use the same box; the grouped hairline box is only for the search suggestions dropdown. (#493)
- **Tappable rows.** Photo, title, one muted line, chevron on the right (`.jend`, `.pj`).
- **Photo rows.** Horizontal rows of small photo cards: photo, name, at most one small line below. Badges go under the name, never on the photo.
- **Sideways rows.** On every page with a sideways row the pictures are sized by what is visible: 2.75 species pictures (two whole and most of a third), 2.25 place pictures, 4:3. The cut-off last picture shows it scrolls. (#521)
- **Section headings.** Small mono caps label (`.jp h3`). Same on every page.
- **Share.** Journey, place and food-web screens each have one share icon (`shareBtn()`), top-right in the same row as the back link. Never a text button.
- **Closed bottom sheet.** Any bottom sheet in its closed state (landing place card, species sheet) is the same peek card: square photo on the left, small uppercase mono kind line, bold name, chevron on the right, no grab handle. Never a bare title bar.
- **Sources & credits.** Every content screen (journey, place, species sheet) ends with one collapsed "Sources & credits" section built by `footHTML()`. Inside, labelled groups always appear in this order and only when they have content: **Sources** (links), **Photos** (one line each: "Photo: author · licence · Wikimedia Commons"). When Commons gives no author the line reads "Photo: author unknown · licence · Wikimedia Commons" (never "Unknown" as a name), **Notes** (plain text), **Evidence** (species sheet only). Lists use the same bullet style everywhere. No separate "More" or "Read more". Controls such as the trace toggle sit above it, not inside.
- **Credits.** Every photo has its author and licence reachable on the screen that shows it. The last link names where the picture came from ("Wikimedia Commons" or "GBIF").

## Screens
Each screen uses the components above and states only what is different about it.
- **Places tab (#568, owner decided 9 and 10 Nov).** On a phone with the sheet open (half or full), a continent, country or state has no title or trail line above the list: the sheet shows one row of pills. The row starts with "‹ <parent>" (one level up) and the selected "All <region> N"; nothing in the row is fixed: "‹ <parent>", "All <region> N" and the children (continents, countries, states) scroll together as one row (owner, 10 Nov, #586). The world has no lead pill. The trail line (World › Africa …, name, count) shows only while the sheet is collapsed. Desktop keeps the breadcrumb. Map mode uses the same pills (built by `chipsHTML()`).
- **Species sheet.** Photo on the left, kind and Latin name on the right, one line, then photo rows (Eats, Eaten by, Also found in places), More, and What if last.
- **Species page.** The place page's top: back link ("‹ Search" when a search is on, else "‹ Places") and one share icon, then a hero with a small photo on the left (the hosted thumbnail, or the species' own icon when there is none), the kind line (mono caps: the level, e.g. "Top predator"), the usual name (h2) and the Latin name in italics. Then the description (with its Wikipedia link) when the species has one, and one collapsed Sources & credits. A species with no description or no picture shows what it has; a missing file shows the name made from the address. Then "Found in N places" (h3) with up to ten tappable place rows (the journey-row look: photo or icon, muted line with the state or country, name, chevron; anchors, not buttons), "Showing 10 of N" when there are more, and a plain link "Show them on the map". No buttons. (#420)
- **Species page sections.** What it eats, What eats it and Related species are one sideways photo row each (`.prow` of `.pmc` cards, the same as the place page), not tall lists. Found in is a sideways row of place cards too, up to 10, then the Show on the map link (#494). (#484)
- **Species wide photo.** The 400 px picture is shown whole (`object-fit: contain`, at most 320px tall), never cropped, so the head of an animal is never cut off. (#485)
- **Species tab.** The third tab, between Places and Journeys, same segmented control. Its first screen is one bordered list of the dropdown's rows, the species found in the most places (no heading line: the pills above carry the names and counts, #534): small photo or leaf, name, "Species · in N places", chevron, at least 44 px. A row opens the species page (Back says "‹ Species" and returns to the tab). Under the list, a "Browse by group" heading and one row per group (name, "N species", chevron). A group opens in the tab: the same "‹ Species" back link as everywhere (`.jback`), its rows (no heading: the selected pill says the name and the count), and a plain "Show N more ›" link for the next 250. While a search is on, one note line sits under the tabs (`#qnote`, the small mono-caps line) and says what the open tab found in the same words on all three tabs: "N places match “x”", "N species match “x”", "N journeys match “x”" ("1 place matches", "No journeys match"); it says "Searching…" while the answer is on its way and "Search is limited right now" when the index could not be had. The header line `#stats` is hidden during a search so the same sentence is not said twice. Every tab label carries what it found ("Places 12", "Species 3", "Journeys 0"; "40+" when the species list is cut), the Journeys tab lists only the journeys whose card has every typed word, and the counts go when the search is cleared. "Show them on the map" always lands on the Places tab. On the species page a small line under the Latin name gives group › order › family ("Birds › Accipitriformes › Pandionidae"); the group is a plain link that opens its list in the Species tab. Below the description the page lists "What it eats" and "What eats it" (up to four species each, the links found in the most places first) in the same rows as the Species tab; a row opens that species and Back says this species' name. "Related species" lists up to five others of the same family (found in the most places first) in the same rows. A species with a larger picture (400 px copy, #421) shows it as a wide photo at the top of the page (16:10, at most 260 px high) and drops the small square; the others keep the small square. A species row shows its picture wherever it appears (dropdown, tab list, tab search). While the tab is open the search box looks for species only: the tab's list becomes "N species match “text”" over the same rows (or "No species match “text”" with a Clear link), and the dropdown lists species only. Clear brings the tab back. The places list, its filters and the result header are hidden while the tab is open. (#422)
- **Species tab groups.** The groups are sideways pills at the top, in the same row and style as the continents on Places ("All" first, a count on each, the picked one marked); the tab no longer ends with a Browse by group list. The same row goes down the taxonomy (order, then family) in the next step, as continent goes to country to state. The Places continent, country and state choice does not show on the Species tab. Order (#519 level 2, owner agreed 9 Nov): inside a group that has two or more orders the one row becomes its orders: "‹ Groups" (back to the group row), "All <group>" with its count, then each order with its count, most species first; the picked pill is marked; an order lists only its species, with no heading line (the selected pill says the name and the count, #534); a group with one order has no order row. Family goes one level further down in the same way. (#519)
- **One row of pills on the Species tab.** The Species tab shows exactly one row of pills (its groups, and later order and family in the same row). The Places region (trail and count in the sheet title) and the habitat chips over the map do not follow you to this tab; the title reads "Connected Earth". Places keeps its region when you return. Only "Show on the map" brings place filters back (continent, country, state). (#533)
- **Eight groups and everyday order names (#534, owner decisions of 11 Nov, recorded in the item).** The first level on the Species tab is eight flat one-word pills after All: Mammals, Birds, Reptiles, Amphibians, Fish, Insects, Plants, Other (other animals, algae and others, and fungi); there is no Animals or Plants level above them. The second level (orders) uses everyday names where one exists (Rodents, Songbirds, Butterflies and moths) for the biggest orders, from a table in the build (`ORDER_NAMES`); an order not in the table keeps its Latin name.
- **At most 8 pills in a row (#534, owner decisions of 11 Nov; applies to every pill row).** A row holds at most 8 pills counting All: All, the 6 biggest by species count and a last pill "More ˅" (a row with no All, such as the world's continents: the 7 biggest and More). More opens a vertical list under the row (the search result row style) of every choice in the row, All first, not only the ones left out, so nothing seems missing. Each list row has an empty circle on its left (pick one) and the count on its right; the current choice has a filled circle with a check, bold text and a tint. A right chevron is not used there: in this app a chevron opens a page, and a pick only filters. Picking closes the list. The selected pill, visible or hidden, always comes first after the ‹ back pill and All (position 2 or 3), so it shows without sideways scrolling; the pill it displaces moves along and the last visible one drops into More; it is an ordinary selected pill (name and count, no chevron), and a selected Other may sit next to All Other (owner, 11 Nov). With no selection the row keeps its natural order, Other last. The More pill always reads "More" and is the only pill with a chevron, so a category never looks like a dropdown (owner, 11 Nov). Every pill in the row counts: the ‹ back pill, All and More included, so a row with a back pill shows ‹, All, 5 categories and More (owner, 11 Nov). The code is `M.capPills` in `src/app/map-view.js`, used by the Places, Journeys and Species rows.
- **Few species make no pill; Other is last (#534, owner decisions of 11 Nov; applies to every level of the Species tab).** A group, order or (later) family needs at least 5 species (`TAB_MIN` in `build/build.py`) to be a pill of its own; the species of a smaller one, and those without an order, join the Other of the level above (for a group too small: the group Other). Other is always the last category in a row and the last row of the More list, so "All Other" and "Other" are never next to each other. The build does both, the app only draws what the files hold. This minimum is for species only: Places keeps every region (continent, country, state) as its own pill, because people look a region up by name and "Other countries" is not a place; a long Places row is handled by the 8-pill cap and the More list (owner, 11 Nov).
- **Map mode.** "Show on the map" from a species opens a titled screen: the search box is empty, the tabs and the count note are hidden, and the one line says "‹ Species · in N places" with the ✕ at its right end, and no "Species page ›" link: it wrapped to a second line on a phone, and "‹ Species" leads back to the search header or the species page it was opened from, both of which link to it (owner, 9 Nov). The "‹ Species" link goes back to where it was opened from: the search that was typed, or the species page. A ✕ at the right end of the title row closes the map mode and goes to the start (the Places tab, the whole world, no search and no species filter); it is not another way back, "‹ Species" still returns to where the map was opened from (owner, 9 Nov). The line is the top row of the list area; under it is one row of pills and no separate trail line: at a continent or country the row starts with "‹ <parent>" (one level up) and a selected "All <region> N" pill, nothing fixed: "‹ <parent>", "All <region> N" and the children (continents, countries, states) scroll together as one row (owner, 10 Nov, #586); at the world level there is no lead pill (owner, #568, 9 Nov); when every place is in one continent the continent level is skipped both ways: the pills start at the countries and "‹" from a country says "World" and returns to those country pills (owner, 12 Nov); nothing sits above the title but the sheet handle; picking a continent, country or state narrows the places and its "N places" follows (owner, #528). The code is in the lazy chunk. (#503, owner decided)
- **Journey page (built; moved from `docs/JOURNEYS.md`, 10 Nov).** The Journeys tab lists curated routes (one file each in `data/journeys/<id>.json`; while searching it shows matching journey counts). A journey opens from its card, a route tapped on the map, or "Read the journey" in a linked food web, at `#j=<id>`. Each file has `text`, `when`, `matters` and `sources`. The route is an SVG map drawn from the country outlines, so it works offline. In order: (1) header with route and photo, e.g. "Osprey · Yellowstone → Amazon"; (2) the story, 3-4 short lines on why it travels (food, breeding, weather) and what happens if it is lost; (3) the map: the route as an arc, the map itself is the play control ("Follow the route"), and when it travels (e.g. leaves in autumn, returns in spring); (4) both ends: a small card for each place with the species' spot in its food web, tap to open it; (5) the sources behind the story; (6) a next journey (related ones, e.g. other birds on the same route). Where the code and this entry differ, the code in `src/app/journeys.js` is the truth; update this entry.
- **Search and species.** How the search box, suggestions, results, species page and Species tab behave is in `docs/DESIGN-SEARCH.md` (agreed, #407). Follow it for any search or species screen.
- **Explore.** Graph controls: None by default: the environment row is always shown, there is no Web mode, and sharing lives on the place page. A one-line caption under the graph replaces the key. Fit appears only after the graph has been moved, and Clear changes only while What if has changes. Explore with nothing selected is just the graph. The sheet or side panel appears only for a selected species.

## Behaviour
- **Navigation shape.** Map → Place page → Explore (graph) → species sheet. Back always goes one step up and says where. Journeys sit beside places and link both ways.
- **One count in a search.** While a search is on, the count is said once, in the note under the tabs. The sheet's title line ("Connected Earth, N places") shows only its handle (a region trail stays), Journeys shows one message not two, and its continent chips count and list only the matching journeys (#495). Nothing else repeats it (#502): the "No places or species match" box, the Journeys intro and its "N journeys" line are hidden during a search, and the species header ("found in N places") shows on Places and Species, not on Journeys. (#496)
- **Count line spacing.** The note under the tabs (`.qnote`, "12 places match “elk”") has 0 above (it sits close under the tabs) and 10px below, on every tab and size (#509). (#482)
- **Map fit with the sheet open.** On a phone the map fits search results and regions into the part of the screen the sheet does not cover: bottom padding is half the screen while the sheet is half or full open, 110px when it is closed. (#498)

## How this is enforced
- `tests/consistency.test.mjs` checks that every screen has exactly one back link with the same look and position.
- When adding a screen, reuse the pattern's existing class rather than writing new CSS.
