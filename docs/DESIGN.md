# Connected Earth: design rules

One place for the UX rules, so the app feels like one app. Read this before any UI work and update it when a rule changes. If a screen needs to break a rule, change the rule here first.

## Principles
1. **Consistency over novelty.** New designs are welcome, but the same thing looks and behaves the same everywhere.
2. **Mobile and desktop stay in sync.** Only layout-dependent parts differ; every improvement applies to both.
3. **Self-explanatory.** No "tap this" hints, no jargon. If it needs a hint, redesign it.
4. **Mobile screen space is premium.** Prefer one line over a card, and nothing permanent that covers content.
5. **Avoid buttons.** Prefer plain links, and tappable cards or rows with a chevron.
6. **Photos, not icons,** wherever a photo exists. Icons only as the fallback while a photo loads or is missing.

## Patterns (one definition each)
- **Back.** One link: accent colour, 16px / 600, "‹ " plus the name of where it goes ("‹ Places", "‹ Journeys", "‹ Greater Yellowstone"). Explore always goes back up to its place page, even after a refresh. 44px tall, top-left of the content on every screen: 24px from the edge on a phone; on desktop the edge of the page column (journeys, places) or of the full-width graph (explore). Over the graph it gets a faint backdrop. Never an arrow-only icon, never a ✕. Code: `.jback` (pages) and `#pbk` (explore); both must look identical (a test checks it).
- **No ✕ on the species sheet.** It closes by tapping empty graph, pressing Esc, or the chevron on phone. A ✕, if ever needed, only dismisses something on top of a screen and never navigates.
- **Clear in the search box.** A small ✕ inside the right end of the field, shown only when there is text, 44px tap area; it empties the field and keeps focus. It is the one ✕ that is not on a sheet, because it navigates nowhere and every phone user expects it there (#409).
- **Species sheet.** Photo on the left, kind and Latin name on the right, one line, then photo rows (Eats, Eaten by, Also found in places), More, and What if last.
- **Species page (#420).** The place page's top: back link ("‹ Search" when a search is on, else "‹ Places") and one share icon, then a hero with a small photo on the left (the hosted thumbnail, or the species' own icon when there is none), the kind line (mono caps: the level, e.g. "Top predator"), the usual name (h2) and the Latin name in italics. Then the description (with its Wikipedia link) when the species has one, and one collapsed Sources & credits. A species with no description or no picture shows what it has; a missing file shows the name made from the address. Then "Found in N places" (h3) with up to ten tappable place rows (the journey-row look: photo or icon, muted line with the state or country, name, chevron; anchors, not buttons), "Showing 10 of N" when there are more, and a plain link "Show them on the map". No buttons.
- **Result header (#425).** One muted line above the list (`#qhead`) when the search text is exactly one species or place: bold name, "found in N places" and a plain link "Species page ›", or "Open the place ›". After "Show on the map" it is the filter chip: "Places with <b>Osprey</b> ✕" (the ✕ is a link that clears the search). A card of a species' place carries one muted line "Has osprey". Links, not buttons.
- **Species tab (#422).** The third tab, between Places and Journeys, same segmented control. Its first screen is a small mono-caps heading ("Found in the most places") over one bordered list of the dropdown's rows: small photo or leaf, name, "Species · in N places", chevron, at least 44 px. A row opens the species page (Back says "‹ Species" and returns to the tab). Under the list, a "Browse by group" heading and one row per group (name, "N species", chevron). A group opens in the tab: the same "‹ Species" back link as everywhere (`.jback`), a heading "Name · N", its rows, and a plain "Show N more ›" link for the next 250. While a search is on, one note line sits under the tabs (`#qnote`, the small mono-caps line) and says what the open tab found in the same words on all three tabs: "N places match “x”", "N species match “x”", "N journeys match “x”" ("1 place matches", "No journeys match"); it says "Searching…" while the answer is on its way and "Search is limited right now" when the index could not be had. The header line `#stats` is hidden during a search so the same sentence is not said twice. Every tab label carries what it found ("Places 12", "Species 3", "Journeys 0"; "40+" when the species list is cut), the Journeys tab lists only the journeys whose card has every typed word, and the counts go when the search is cleared. "Show them on the map" always lands on the Places tab. On the species page a small line under the Latin name gives group › order › family ("Birds › Accipitriformes › Pandionidae"); the group is a plain link that opens its list in the Species tab. Below the description the page lists "What it eats" and "What eats it" (up to four species each, the links found in the most places first) in the same rows as the Species tab; a row opens that species and Back says this species' name. "Related species" lists up to five others of the same family (found in the most places first) in the same rows. A species with a larger picture (400 px copy, #421) shows it as a wide photo at the top of the page (16:10, at most 260 px high) and drops the small square; the others keep the small square. A species row shows its picture wherever it appears (dropdown, tab list, tab search). While the tab is open the search box looks for species only: the tab's list becomes "N species match “text”" over the same rows (or "No species match “text”" with a Clear link), and the dropdown lists species only. Clear brings the tab back. The places list, its filters and the result header are hidden while the tab is open.
- **Species page sections (#484).** What it eats, What eats it and Related species are one sideways photo row each (`.prow` of `.pmc` cards, the same as the place page), not tall lists. Found in is a sideways row of place cards too, up to 10, then the Show on the map link (#494).
- **Species wide photo (#485).** The 400 px picture is shown whole (`object-fit: contain`, at most 320px tall), never cropped, so the head of an animal is never cut off.
- **Result row (#483).** Places, Species and Journeys use one row in lists and search results: 52px square picture (or the fallback icon) on the left, the title (16px, 700) first, one muted line below in plain text (not capitals), a chevron on the right. A Places row has a second muted line, "N species · N feeding links" (#508). Places and Journeys keep their own list container; only the row look is shared. Blurbs, counts and the "Open the food web" link are not in the row.
- **One list format (#493).** Rows on all three tabs are separate rounded cards: 1px line, 12px corners, 10px padding, 8px between rows, 52px picture, 16px title, one muted line, a chevron. Places, Journeys and Species use the same box; the grouped hairline box is only for the search suggestions dropdown.
- **Map mode (#503, owner decided).** "Show on the map" from a species opens a titled screen: the search box is empty, the tabs and the count note are hidden, and the one line says "‹ Species · in N places · Species page ›". The "‹ Species" link goes back to where it was opened from: the search that was typed, or the species page. No second ✕. The code is in the lazy chunk.
- **Sideways rows (#521).** On every page with a sideways row the pictures are sized by what is visible: 2.75 species pictures (two whole and most of a third), 2.25 place pictures, 4:3. The cut-off last picture shows it scrolls.
- **Species tab groups (#519).** The groups are sideways pills at the top, in the same row and style as the continents on Places ("All" first, a count on each, the picked one marked); the tab no longer ends with a Browse by group list. The same row goes down the taxonomy (order, then family) in the next step, as continent goes to country to state. The Places continent, country and state choice does not show on the Species tab.
- **Capitals (#481, owner: be frugal).** Capital letters are forced (`text-transform: uppercase`) in three roles only: short kind labels and badges (`.tag`, `.badge`, `.tlpill`, `.jkind`, `.eyebrow`), section heading labels (`h3`/`h4`, "Found in") and map labels. Everything else is plain text in normal case: the count and note lines (`.sub`), one-line row descriptions, hints, counts, and the More and Sources & credits links. A new style must not add capitals outside those roles.
- **Count line spacing (#482).** The note under the tabs (`.qnote`, "12 places match “elk”") has 0 above (it sits close under the tabs) and 10px below, on every tab and size (#509).
- **One count in a search (#496).** While a search is on, the count is said once, in the note under the tabs. The sheet's title line ("Connected Earth, N places") shows only its handle (a region trail stays), Journeys shows one message not two, and its continent chips count and list only the matching journeys (#495). Nothing else repeats it (#502): the "No places or species match" box, the Journeys intro and its "N journeys" line are hidden during a search, and the species header ("found in N places") shows on Places and Species, not on Journeys.
- **Map fit with the sheet open (#498).** On a phone the map fits search results and regions into the part of the screen the sheet does not cover: bottom padding is half the screen while the sheet is half or full open, 110px when it is closed.
- **Section headings.** Small mono caps label (`.jp h3`). Same on every page.
- **Story blocks.** "The story" is plain text with a bold lead-in; "Why it matters" has the accent bar (`.jwhy`). Journeys and places use the same blocks.
- **Text length.** On a place page, "The story" is at most 60 words and "Why it matters" at most 40, so both fit a phone screen without scrolling. Anything longer goes in the sources, not the text. `tests/textlength.test.mjs` enforces it.
- **Place summary.** A state park or refuge with no story shows one plain sentence from the first line of its Wikipedia article (no lead-in, same spot and look as the story). At most 40 words, with brackets removed, and none when the article is a disambiguation page or about something else. The Wikipedia article is credited in the place's Sources & credits. `tests/textlength.test.mjs` enforces it.
- **Photo rows.** Horizontal rows of small photo cards: photo, name, at most one small line below. Badges go under the name, never on the photo.
- **Tappable rows.** Photo, title, one muted line, chevron on the right (`.jend`, `.pj`).
- **Fonts.** Display font for names and titles, body font for text, mono for small labels. Sizes: page title 24, row title 16, body 16, card name 12–13, label 11, fine print 12.
- **Credits.** Every photo has its author and licence reachable on the screen that shows it.
- **Start screen.** Logo, name, tagline and a thin progress bar on a dark ocean blue (`#boot`, `#0b2230`) with light text, in light and dark mode alike, close to the satellite map's opening look so the hand-over to the map is not a flash from white. Removed (fade) when the map has drawn.
- **Share.** Journey, place and food-web screens each have one share icon (`shareBtn()`), top-right in the same row as the back link. Never a text button.
- **Closed bottom sheet.** Any bottom sheet in its closed state (landing place card, species sheet) is the same peek card: square photo on the left, small uppercase mono kind line, bold name, chevron on the right, no grab handle. Never a bare title bar.
- **Sources & credits.** Every content screen (journey, place, species sheet) ends with one collapsed "Sources & credits" section built by `footHTML()`. Inside, labelled groups always appear in this order and only when they have content: **Sources** (links), **Photos** (one line each: "Photo: author · licence · Wikimedia Commons"). When Commons gives no author the line reads "Photo: author unknown · licence · Wikimedia Commons" (never "Unknown" as a name), **Notes** (plain text), **Evidence** (species sheet only). Lists use the same bullet style everywhere. No separate "More" or "Read more". Controls such as the trace toggle sit above it, not inside.

## Search and species
How the search box, suggestions, results, species page and Species tab behave is in `docs/SEARCH-CX.md` (agreed, #407). Follow it for any search or species screen.

## Navigation shape
Map → Place page → Explore (graph) → species sheet. Back always goes one step up and says where. Journeys sit beside places and link both ways.
- **Graph controls.** None by default: the environment row is always shown, there is no Web mode, and sharing lives on the place page. A one-line caption under the graph replaces the key. Fit appears only after the graph has been moved, and Clear changes only while What if has changes.
- Explore with nothing selected is just the graph. The sheet or side panel appears only for a selected species.

## How this is enforced
- `tests/consistency.test.mjs` checks that every screen has exactly one back link with the same look and position.
- When adding a screen, reuse the pattern's existing class rather than writing new CSS.
