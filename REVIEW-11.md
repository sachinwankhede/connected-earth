## Faver-Dykes State Park (`flfaverdykes`)

## Highlands Hammock State Park (`flhighlandshammock`)

**Story** (37 words): Highlands Hammock State Park covers 9,000 acres in Highlands County, Florida, west of Sebring. Opened in 1931, it features old-growth forest with an old-growth bald cypress swamp and trees believed to be over a thousand years old.

**Why it matters** (26 words): Part of the natural area is recognized by the Old-Growth Forest Network. The park shelters wildlife including white-tailed deer, American alligators, and the rare Florida panther.

## Hillsborough River State Park (`flhillsboroughrive`)

**Story** (42 words): Hillsborough River State Park is located in Florida, USA, covering over 1,369 hectares or 3,383 acres. Created in 1938, it is one of the eight original Florida State Parks and features a swiftly flowing section of the Hillsborough River with small rapids.

**Why it matters** (30 words): The park is cleaved into two halves by the Hillsborough River, which is a blackwater river and one of the few rivers in Florida to have a system of rapids.

## John Pennekamp Coral Reef State Park (`fljohnpennekampcor`)

**Story** (50 words): John Pennekamp Coral Reef State Park is located on Key Largo in Florida and was the first underwater park in the United States. It opened in 1963 and includes around 70 square nautical miles of adjacent Atlantic Ocean waters. The primary attractions are the coral reefs and associated marine life.

**Why it matters** (29 words): The Florida Keys are among the only living coral reef formations in the continental United States. The park helps protect these reefs from damage and preserves their marine life.

## Jonathan Dickinson State Park (`fljonathandickinso`)

**Why it matters** (33 words): The park is home to many significant and endangered species, including barking tree frogs, pine woods tree frogs, and Florida scrub jays. Its wetlands have more species of amphibians than the Everglades do.

## Manatee Springs State Park (`flmanateesprings`)

## Torreya State Park (`fltorreya`)

**Story** (47 words): Torreya State Park is a 13,735 acres Florida State Park located north of S.R 12 on the Apalachicola River, in northwestern Florida. It has one of the most variable terrains of any in Florida, featuring river swamps, high pinelands, extensive ravines, and high bluffs along the river.

**Why it matters** (38 words): It is named for the Florida Nutmeg trees, a rare species of Torreya tree endemic to the local east bank of the Apalachicola River's limestone bluffs, and serves as a home for endangered species like the few-flowered croomia.

## Wekiwa Springs State Park (`flwekiwasprings`)

## Black Rock Mountain State Park (`gablackrockmountai`)

**Story** (44 words): Black Rock Mountain State Park covers 1,743 acres in Rabun County, Georgia, in the Blue Ridge Mountains. Established in 1952, it is the state's highest state park and features sheer cliffs of dark-colored biotite gneiss, four peaks over 3,000 feet, and Black Rock Lake.

**Why it matters** (39 words): The park provides hiking trails through forests and mountain streams that connect to the Eastern Continental Divide and flow into the Chattooga River. It shelters diverse plant-filled coves and offers vistas extending into the Great Smoky Mountains National Park.
